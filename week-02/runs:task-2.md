### You

You are writing bean description cards for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription.
Our voice is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent.
Wildframe is adding three coffees to its shop, and every coffee gets a bean card prepared in the same format.
Here is one finished card. Match its layout exactly: the same five lines, labels, and order.
HUILA, COLOMBIA
Roast: Medium
Tastes like: red apple, brown sugar, cocoa
Brew it: Pour-over or drip
Real talk: The coffee that converted our office's tea drinker. Mostly.
Write a card in this layout for each of these coffees:
- Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. Light roast. Notes: jasmine, lemon, bergamot. Best as pour-over.
- Cerrado, Brazil. Medium-dark roast. Notes: peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity. Good for espresso or French press.
- Chiapas, Mexico. Medium roast. Notes: almond, caramel, orange peel. Good for drip or AeroPress.
Return only the cards, with a blank line between them.

### Claude

YIRGACHEFFE, ETHIOPIA
Roast: Light
Tastes like: jasmine, lemon, bergamot
Brew it: Pour-over
Real talk: Smells like a flower shop, tastes like you made a good life choice this morning.

CERRADO, BRAZIL
Roast: Medium-dark
Tastes like: peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity
Brew it: Espresso or French press
Real talk: Smooth enough to drink black, sweet enough to make you forget you meant to add sugar.

CHIAPAS, MEXICO
Roast: Medium
Tastes like: almond, caramel, orange peel
Brew it: Drip or AeroPress
Real talk: The reliable friend who always shows up and never makes it weird.
