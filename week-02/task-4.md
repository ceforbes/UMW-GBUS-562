### You

You are a marketing analyst for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Wildframe can run one four-week pop-up stand this spring, with a total rent budget of $5,000 for the four weeks. 
Pop-up location data from comparable past events:
- Riverside Farmers Market: 9,000 visitors per week; 2% of visitors buy; average sale $14 (mostly whole-bean bags); rent $900 per week.
- Union Station concourse: 30,000 visitors per week; 0.5% of visitors buy; average sale $6 (mostly single cups); rent $2,000 per week.
- Campus library atrium: 6,000 visitors per week; 3% of visitors buy; average sale $5 (single cups); rent $600 per week.
Work through this step by step before recommending:
1. Estimate expected purchases from each location.
2. Judge how well the brand would be promoted at each location.
3. Weigh those factors against each other.
Then give your recommendation in one sentence, written so that the sentence begins with "Recommendation:" followed by the location's name.

### Claude

# Step 1: Estimate Expected Purchases & Revenue (per week, then x4 weeks)

| Location | Visitors/wk | Conv. Rate | Buyers/wk | Avg Sale | Revenue/wk | Rent/wk | 4-Wk Rent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Riverside | 9,000 | 2% | 180 | $14 | $2,520 | $900 | $3,600 |
| Union Station | 30,000 | 0.5% | 150 | $6 | $900 | $2,000 | **$8,000** |
| Campus Library | 6,000 | 3% | 180 | $5 | $900 | $600 | $2,400 |

**Critical issue:** Union Station's 4-week rent ($8,000) blows past the $5,000 total budget outright — it's not a viable option for a full four-week run.

Comparing the two budget-feasible options over 4 weeks:
- **Riverside:** 720 buyers, $10,080 revenue, $3,600 rent → ~$6,480 gross margin, $1,400 of budget left over
- **Campus Library:** 720 buyers, $3,600 revenue, $2,400 rent → ~$1,200 gross margin, $2,600 of budget left over

Same buyer volume, but Riverside generates nearly 3x the revenue because customers are buying whole-bean bags rather than single cups.

# Step 2: Judge Brand Promotion Value

- **Riverside Farmers Market:** Whole-bean buyers are closer to Wildframe's core subscription customer — people buying beans to brew at home are natural subscription leads. Farmers market shoppers also tend to value local, artisanal brands and have time for conversation/sampling, which supports brand storytelling and loyalty-building.
- **Union Station:** High foot traffic but transactional, grab-and-go commuters with little interest in brand engagement or subscriptions; also disqualified on budget.
- **Campus Library:** Good trial exposure and high conversion rate, but single-cup buyers are price-sensitive students, a narrower demographic less likely to convert into paying monthly subscribers.

# Step 3: Weigh the Factors

- Union Station is eliminated immediately — it doesn't fit the budget constraint for a four-week run.
- Between the two remaining options, Riverside and Campus Library produce identical buyer counts, but Riverside's customers spend far more per visit and are a stronger match with Wildframe's whole-bean/subscription business model.
- Riverside also leaves a comfortable budget cushion while nearly tripling expected revenue compared to Campus Library, making it the stronger choice both financially and for brand-building toward subscription growth.

**Recommendation:** Riverside Farmers Market.
